1.The online leasing service fetches the credit rating for the contract from the rating agency website.

2.The rating agency website generates a credit rating report for the online leasing service.

3.The online leasing service creates a risk assessment for the contract and from the credit rating report in the risk management system.

4.The online leasing service notifies the risk manager about the credit rating.

5.The risk manager decides on a voting result for the risk assessment in the risk management system.

6.The risk manager signs the voted contract in the online leasing service.
