1.The customer chooses a specific car from the catalog.

2.The customer asks the salesperson for an offer.

3.The salesperson offers a contract for a car with a monthly installment to the customer.

4.The customer signs the contract with the salesperson.

5.The salesperson passes the contract on to the risk manager.

6.The risk manager assesses the risk for the contract.

7.The risk manager votes on the contract.

8.The risk manager informs the salesperson about the voting result.

9.The salesperson hands over the car to the customer.

10.The customer pays the monthly installment regularly.

11.The customer returns the car to the salesperson after the leasing period.
