1.The risk manager fills out the credit rating form with information from the contract on the rating agency website.

2.The rating agency website generates a credit rating report and provides it to the risk manager.

3.The risk manager creates a risk assessment for the contract based on the credit rating report and stores the risk assessment in the risk management system.

4.The risk manager decides on a voting result for the risk assessment, and the decision is stored in the risk management system.

5.The risk manager passes the voted contract on to the sales person.

6.The sales person signs the voted contract.
