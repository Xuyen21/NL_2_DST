Credit risk assessment for contract


1.The risk manager fills out the credit rating form with information from the contract on the rating agency website.

2.The rating agency website generates a credit rating report for the risk manager.

3.The risk manager creates a credit rating report for the risk manager.

4. The risk manager creates risk assessment for the contract with
and stores the risk assessment in the risk management system.
(notes: risk assessment needs to be present in the system, because only then the voting result can be entered)


4.The risk manager decides on a voting result for the risk assessment, and the decision is stored in the risk management system.

5.The risk manager passes the voted contract on to the sales person.(notes: risk assessment needs to be present in the system,
because only then the voting result can be entered)

6.The sales person signs the voted contract.
