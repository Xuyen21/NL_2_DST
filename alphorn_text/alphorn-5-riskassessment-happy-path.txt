1.The online leasing service fetches the customer’s credit rating for the contract from the rating agency website.

2.The rating agency website generates a credit rating report and provides it to the online leasing service.

3.The online leasing service creates a risk assessment for the contract based on the credit rating report and stores the risk assessment in the risk management system.

4.The online leasing service notifies the risk manager about the credit rating.

5.The risk manager decides on a voting result for the risk assessment, and the decision is stored in the risk management system.

6.The risk manager signs the voted contract in the online leasing service.
